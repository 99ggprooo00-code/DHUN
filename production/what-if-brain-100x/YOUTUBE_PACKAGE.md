## 2. Video description

What if you woke up one morning and everyone around you was suddenly moving in slow motion? Your brain is running a hundred times faster than it did yesterday — and there is no off switch.

In this animated stickman story, a quiet student becomes untouchable: he finishes an exam in four minutes, out-thinks a chess master in eleven moves, predicts a bus delay before it happens, and saves a stranger from a falling sign because he noticed one rusted bolt. For a while, it looks like a gift.

Then the speed follows him home. Every conversation stretches into hours inside his head. Every joke, every story, every "how was school" becomes a slow, unbearable blur. On the night his little sister asks him the only question he can't answer, he has to choose between the mind that makes him extraordinary and the people who made him human.

This is a short animated sci-fi story about intelligence, isolation, and the cost of being the smartest person in the room.

*What would you choose — the gift, or the people? Tell me in the comments.*

**Clarification:** This is a fictional "what if" story, not a claim about real human cognition. A brain running 100x faster isn't possible, and real giftedness doesn't look like this — the story uses exaggeration to explore isolation, not neuroscience.
